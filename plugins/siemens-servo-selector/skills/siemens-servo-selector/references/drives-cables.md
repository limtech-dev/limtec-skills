## Cables

- **V90**: MOTION-CONNECT 300 — power `6FX3002-5CK01 / 5CL02 / 5CL12-<len>`; encoder `2DB1x/2DB2x` (absolute) or `2CT1x/2CT2x` (incremental); brake `5BL03/5BK02`.
- **S200**: MOTION-CONNECT 350 — `6FX3502-7CD01-<len>`, one combined power+encoder cable.
- **S200 Compact**: power `6FX1002-5DN32-<len>` (brake cores included); encoder `6FX1002-2XB12-<len>` / `2XB14-<len>` (with battery).
- **S210**: **OCC single cable** (power + encoder + brake in one) — SH20 → `6FX5002-8QN04`; SH30–52 → `6FX5002-8QN08` (0.75 mm², M17); SH63+ → `6FX5002-8QN11` (1.5 mm², M23), long-run / derated `8QN21` (2.5 mm²). **Max 50 m**; the built-in filter guarantees C3 only to 25 m.
- **S120**: power `6FX5002-5DG23-…`; DRIVE-CLiQ `6FX5002-2DC10-…` (long) / `6FX2002-1DC…` (short).

## Cable length code

Last block = `1<decametre><metre>0`. Decametre: A=0 B=10 C=20 D=30 E=40 **F=50**. Metre: A=0 B=1 C=2 D=3 E=4 F=5 G=6 H=7 J=8 K=9 (letter I is skipped).
Examples: 3 m = `1AD0`, 5 m = `1AF0`, 10 m = `1BA0`, 15 m = `1BF0`, 25 m = `1CF0`, **50 m = `1FA0`**.

Never guess a length code — 50 m is `1FA0`, not `1FA` or `1F0`.
