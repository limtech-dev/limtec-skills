The series

Six jack families cover machine screw and ball screw drives, foot or trunnion mounting, and single- or multi-input shafts.

What the selector resolves

Every position of the order code is built by the engine — the same code paths as the online tool.

Motion & mounting

Rotating screw (S) or translating nut (N), foot or trunnion mounting, single (P1) or double (P2) input shaft, right- or left-hand input, flange-end or threaded joints.

Synchronised groups

Two to eight jacks in H or U layouts, with mechanical synchronisation (one motor through gearboxes) or electrical synchronisation. Platform size inputs are supported for multi-jack sets.

Checks before a model is offered

Thrust, speed and power limits, buckling (stability), life and duty cycle, self-locking requirement — an undersized jack does not slip through.

Environment & duty

Indoor, outdoor or cleanroom derating, cycles per day / hours per day / days per year feed the life calculation, and guides (customer-supplied vs. built-in) affect the stability check.

Drive & pricing

AC motor, 220 V or 380 V servo, or hand input (no motor priced). The bill of materials carries USD reference prices for the whole set.

Proposal PDF

Export the selection as an English proposal PDF with layout drawing, checks and pricing — ready to attach to a quotation.

One command, one answer

Four SJA jacks lifting 8 kN each through a 700 mm stroke at 6.25 mm/s, mechanically synchronised, servo driven.

node scripts/select.js --product=screwjack \
  --set=units=4 --set=load=8 --set=load_unit=kN \
  --set=stroke_mm=700 --set=speed_mm_s=6.25 \
  --set=layout=h --set=motion=S --set=guide=yes \
  --set=env=indoor --set=sync=mechanical \
  --set=drive=servo220 --set=series=auto

The demo run resolves this to SJA5 with a four-jack bill of materials totalling USD 1,138 (reference prices). Add --json=result.json for structured output, or --list-fields to see every key for this product line.

Frequently asked

Is the engine the same as the online tool?

Yes. The selection engine and the price book are byte-identical to the Lim-Tec online selector (md5 list shipped in scripts/source_manifest.json). The skill is an offline wrapper, so an offline answer matches the online answer.

Are the prices official offers?

No. They are USD reference prices from the Lim-Tec price system, for evaluation and quoting support. Formal quotations come from Lim-Tec or its sales channels.

Can it size a set of jacks that must stay level?

Yes — that is the synchronised-group case: unit count, H / U layout, mechanical or electrical synchronisation, and platform dimensions. The output prices the full set, including the gearboxes that share one motor.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The skill runs fully offline; only the initial download from GitHub needs a connection.
