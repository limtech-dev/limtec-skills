What the selector resolves

Every position of the order code is built by the engine — the same code paths as the online tool.

Frame & input

Series and frame from thrust, stroke and speed; belt or direct input forms (P10/P20, P15, …); front flange (FF), external thread (FM) and other mounting and rod-end joints.

Servo matching

Picks the motor platform with you — Siemens V90 / S200 / S210 / S120, Delta, Inovance — and the supply (220 V or 380 V servo, or AC motor drive).

Encoder & protection

Incremental, absolute or resolver feedback and the protection class (IP65 typical) are resolved into the order code rather than left to the catalogue.

Checks before a model is offered

Thrust, speed and power limits, buckling (stability), life and duty cycle — the load unit is echoed back explicitly, because tonnes vs. kN is where sizing mistakes happen.

Accessories

Anti-rotation (AR) and other accessory codes are repeatable and priced in the bill of materials.

Proposal PDF

Export the selection as an English proposal PDF with layout, checks and pricing — ready to attach to a quotation.

One command, one answer

A DMB cylinder pushing 1 tonne through a 400 mm stroke at 200 mm/s, Siemens V90 servo on a 380 V drive, anti-rotation rod end.

node scripts/select.js --product=cylinder \
  --set=series=DMB --set=load=1 --set=load_unit=t \
  --set=stroke_mm=400 --set=speed_mm_s=200 \
  --set=input_form=P10/P20 --set=install=FF --set=joint=FM \
  --set=servo_brand=V90 --set=drive=servo380 \
  --set=accessories=AR

The demo run resolves this to DMB35 with a total of USD 1,875 (reference prices). Add --json=result.json for structured output, or --list-fields to see every key for this product line.

Frequently asked

Is the engine the same as the online tool?

Yes. The selection engine and the price book are byte-identical to the Lim-Tec online selector (md5 list shipped in scripts/source_manifest.json). The skill is an offline wrapper, so an offline answer matches the online answer.

Are the prices official offers?

No. They are USD reference prices from the Lim-Tec price system, for evaluation and quoting support. Formal quotations come from Lim-Tec or its sales channels.

Which servo brands can it match?

Siemens V90, S200, S210 and S120, plus Delta and Inovance platforms. The brand choice feeds the motor priced in the bill of materials.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The skill runs fully offline; only the initial download from GitHub needs a connection.
