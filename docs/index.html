What you get back

Not a shortlist — an orderable bill of materials with the reasoning shown.

Motor + drive + cable

Every position of the order number is resolved: motor frame, winding, encoder, shaft end, brake, degree of protection, plus the matching drive and the OCC or power/encoder cable.

Duty-point checks

Rated and maximum torque, speed limits, inertia ratio and drive current are checked against the selected motor, so an undersized axis does not slip through.

Cable length codes

Pick the run length once — the selector builds the correct length suffix (for example a 50 m OCC run) instead of leaving you to decode the catalogue.

Reference prices

USD reference prices for the whole bill of materials, converted from actual transaction records. Formal quotations still come from Siemens or an authorised distributor.

PDF datasheet

Export the selection as a PDF you can attach to a proposal — motor, drive, cables, checks and prices on one page.

No install, no login

Runs in the browser, on desktop or phone. No account, no plug-in, no API key.

How it works

Three inputs, one answer. The tool reads the duty point, walks the model library, and returns the first combination that passes every check.

  1. Describe the axis. Load (N, kN or kg) and stroke, or directly torque and speed. Add brake, encoder type, shaft end, supply voltage and cable length if they are already specified.
  2. Let it size. The selector resolves the motor series, frame and winding, then picks the smallest drive whose current rating covers the motor, then builds the cable order number from the length code.
  3. Take the result. Copy the order numbers into your BOM, or export the PDF datasheet for the customer.

Frequently asked

Is it really free?

Yes. The selector is free to use and requires no account. It is published by Lim-Tec as a service to machine builders and integrators.

Are the prices official Siemens prices?

No. They are reference prices converted from historical transaction records into USD. A formal quotation must come from Siemens or an authorised distributor. Where no price is on record, the tool says “on request” instead of guessing.

Which Siemens servo systems are covered?

SINAMICS V90 with 1FL6 motors, S200 with 1FL2, S200 Compact with 1FL1 (200 V), S210 with 1FK2, and S120 with 1FK7 / 1FT7 / 1PH8. 160 motors in total, 0.05 kW to 100 kW.

Can I use it on a phone on site?

Yes. The layout collapses to a single column below 760 px and the selector works with touch input, so it is usable while standing next to a machine.

Do you keep the data I enter?

The selection runs in your browser. Only messages you deliberately submit through the contact box are stored.
